Tentang Mahligai Seni

Mahligai Seni ialah jenama hiasan rumah Islamik milik SYNAPSIS ENTERPRISE (No. Pendaftaran SSM: SA0163793-U), sebuah perniagaan berdaftar di Malaysia. Kami mereka dan menghasilkan frame kaligrafi, Khat 3D Cantum, frame berlampu LED dan hiasan dinding Islamik menggunakan teknologi potongan laser di bengkel dan galeri kami di Shah Alam, Selangor.

Apa yang kami lakukan

  • Reka bentuk dan pengeluaran hiasan dinding Islamik: kaligrafi, ayat Al-Quran, doa dan Asmaul Husna.
  • Jualan dalam talian melalui kedai rasmi kami di Shopee (lasercutmalaysia) dan jualan terus di galeri kami.
  • Laman web ini ialah katalog produk. Pembelian, pembayaran dan penghantaran diselesaikan di Shopee.

About Mahligai Seni

Mahligai Seni is an Islamic home décor brand owned and operated by SYNAPSIS ENTERPRISE (Malaysian business registration no. SA0163793-U). We design and manufacture calligraphy frames, 3D wall calligraphy (“Khat 3D Cantum”), LED-lit frames and Islamic wall art using laser-cutting technology at our workshop and showroom in Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia.

What we do

  • Design and production of Islamic wall art: calligraphy, Quranic verses, supplications and the 99 Names of Allah.
  • Online sales through our official Shopee store (lasercutmalaysia) and walk-in sales at our showroom.
  • This website is our product catalogue. Orders, payments and delivery are completed on Shopee.
